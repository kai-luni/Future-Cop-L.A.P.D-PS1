## Hardware meaning

`0x1F8010C8` is `DMA4_CHCR`, the channel control register of DMA channel 4, the
SPU channel. `func_800973B4` writes `0x01000200` here — start bit set, sync
mode 2 — after the address and block control words are in place, which starts
the transfer to SPU RAM.
